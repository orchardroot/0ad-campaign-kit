# 0 A.D. Island & Naval Maps — Cheat-Sheet

Every ship-friendly map installed on this machine (0 A.D. 0.28 + mod.io map mods), with settings for a relaxed game. Maps marked *(CM2)* come from the Community Maps 2 mod.

## Easy-game settings (use these every time)

- **AI difficulty:** Very Easy while learning, Easy once comfortable. **AI behavior: Defensive** makes the AI sit at home — ideal for chill games.
- **Ceasefire:** in Match Setup, set Ceasefire to 10–15 minutes for a guaranteed peaceful build-up. On island maps you're already protected by water, so even 5 minutes is plenty.
- **Civilization:** Athens (best all-round navy, cheap arrow ships) or Carthage (superb harbours and trade). Both get strong fishing.
- **Starting resources:** bump to Medium/High if you want to skip early scarcity.
- **Population cap:** 150–200 keeps big island games smooth.
- The rule of every naval map: **whoever controls the water controls the game.** Fish for food, never stop building warships, sink transports on sight.

## Quick recipes

- **Totally chill boat-building afternoon:** Islands (random), 1 AI, Sandbox difficulty. It will never attack.
- **Gentle but real game:** Islands or Archipelago, 1 AI, Very Easy + Defensive, 10-min ceasefire.
- **Team comfort:** Island Stronghold, you + Easy AI ally vs two Very Easy AIs — shared island, shared navy.
- **The spectacle:** Aegean Archipelago 8p, everyone Very Easy, pop cap 150, and let chaos reign.
